# AffiliRank — 3-Minute Explainer Video Script

**Format:** 1920x1080, 30fps · **Target runtime:** 3:00 · **Tone:** Energetic, confident, direct

---

## ACT I — THE PROBLEM (0:00–0:45)

### Scene 1 — Cold Open (0:00–0:15)
**Visual:** Fast montage — a frustrated affiliate staring at a blank blog editor, then a wall of tabs (SEO tools, keyword planners, 20+ open docs). Quick cuts, ~1s each.
**On-screen text:** `STOP BUILDING FROM SCRATCH` (slams in center)

**VO:**
> Let's be real. Affiliate marketing is the hardest way to make easy money — if you build it the old way. Find a product. Write a review. Keyword research. Schema markup. Affiliate links. And then? You wait months hoping Google shows up.

---

### Scene 2 — The Numbers (0:15–0:30)
**Visual:** Animated counter: 80% appears, then 20 hours, then a calendar flying past weeks. Red "waiting" bars.
**On-screen text:** `80% of your time = content` → `only 20% = actually earning`

**VO:**
> Most affiliates burn eighty percent of their time on content and SEO — the stuff that doesn't make money. By the time you've published one article, your competitors have already ranked, and you're still waiting on Google.

---

### Scene 3 — The Old Stack (0:30–0:45)
**Visual:** Split-screen of the "old stack" — blog platform, SEO plugin, keyword tool, schema generator, link shortener — all separate boxes, chaotic arrows between them.
**On-screen text:** `5+ tools. Hours of setup. Months to rank.`

**VO:**
> Now string together the tools you need: a blog platform, an SEO plugin, a keyword research tool, schema markup, affiliate links. Five different tools, hours of setup — and months before your first ranking page.

## PRODUCTION NOTES

- **Music:** upbeat, energetic tech track (~120 BPM). Duck under VO at -14dB.
- **Pacing:** quick cuts in the problem section, slower/confident in the solution section.
- **Captions:** white text, bold, bottom third, 26px min, black outline. One caption at a time — never stacked.
- **Cuts:** ~1s per cut in Act I, 2–3s holds in Act II, punchy end.
- **Total scenes:** 11
- **Total runtime:** ~3:00 (buffers of ±5s for breathers)
